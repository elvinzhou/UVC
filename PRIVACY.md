# Privacy Policy — Borescope

_Last updated: 2026-11-20_

Borescope is a free, open-source viewer for USB (UVC) borescopes and similar USB cameras.

## Summary

**Borescope does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data.**

## What the app does

- It shows live video from a USB camera you plug into your device.
- When you tap the photo or record button, it saves the image or video **on your device**
  (Pictures/Borescope and Movies/Borescope). These files stay on your device unless you
  share them yourself with another app.
- It remembers a few settings (such as the chosen video mode) locally on your device.

## What the app does not do

- It does not connect to the internet. The app does not request the internet permission.
- It has no accounts, advertising, analytics, crash reporting, or third-party tracking code.
- It does not read your contacts, location, microphone, or files other than the photos and
  videos it saves itself.

## Permissions

- **USB device access:** requested by Android when you plug in a camera, so the app can read video from it.
- **Camera:** required by Android to stream video from a USB camera. The app does not use your
  phone's built-in cameras and does not send camera data anywhere.

## Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.
